Free BTU room chart — print it.

Not ACCA Manual J. Oversizing is the #1 DIY mistake; we say so.

Questions

Room air conditioner and dehumidifier sizes taken from the ENERGY STAR charts.

  • What size air conditioner for a 150 sq ft room — 5,000 or 6,000 BTU/hr on the chart line.
  • What size air conditioner for a 200 sq ft room — 6,000 BTU/hr inside the 150 up to 250 row.
  • What size air conditioner for a 250 sq ft room — 6,000 or 7,000 BTU/hr on the chart line.
  • What size air conditioner for a 300 sq ft room — 7,000 or 8,000 BTU/hr on the chart line.
  • What size air conditioner for a 400 sq ft room — 9,000 or 10,000 BTU/hr on the chart line.
  • What size air conditioner for a 500 sq ft room — 12,000 BTU/hr in the 450 up to 550 row.
  • What size air conditioner for 600 sq ft — 14,000 BTU/hr in the 550 up to 700 row.
  • What size air conditioner for 700 sq ft — 14,000 or 18,000 BTU/hr on the chart line.
  • What size air conditioner for 800 sq ft — 18,000 BTU/hr in the 700 up to 1,000 row.
  • What size air conditioner for 1,200 sq ft — 21,000 or 23,000 BTU/hr on the chart line.
  • What size air conditioner for 1,500 sq ft — 24,000 or 30,000 BTU/hr on the chart line.
  • What size air conditioner for 2,000 sq ft — 30,000 or 34,000 BTU/hr on the chart line.
  • What size air conditioner for a kitchen — add 4,000 BTU/hr; a 150 up to 250 sq ft kitchen is 10,000 BTU/hr.
  • What size dehumidifier for a basement — 20–30, 25–40, or 30–50 pints under 2,000 sq ft.
  • What size dehumidifier for 2,000 sq ft — 30+, 40+, or 50+ pints, and better to oversize.
