Understanding Topic map

Oversized AC short-cycles and never dehumidifies. Undersized runs constantly. Topic map requires Manual-J-style load math.

This tool uses the standard industry formula: it starts from the primary input, applies the safety margin, and rounds up to the next standard size. All intermediate steps are shown on the result card so you can verify the math yourself.

Common mistakes to avoid: reading only the peak rating instead of continuous rating, forgetting headroom for future expansion, or skipping the duty-cycle assumption.
