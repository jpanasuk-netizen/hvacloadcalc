Multi-zone vs several single-zone units

For two or three rooms, compare a single-zone unit with a multi-zone unit before you default to multi-zone. This page does not print prices. Multi-zone wins when outdoor space is limited or rooms need simultaneous different setpoints. Multi-zone condensers divide their capacity across active heads, and if all zones call at once each head gets less than its nameplate — one reason pros size multi-zone systems to the sum of room loads with attention to diversity, not just head count.

The oversizing trap, quantified

An oversized fixed-speed unit can satisfy the thermostat and shut off before the room has dried out, which leaves the air cold and clammy and makes the compressor cycle. Inverter mini-splits throttle down instead of shutting off. A head far above the load is capacity you can't use. This page does not print a percent of oversize that is safe.

DIY vs pro install

Pre-charged line-set units have made some single-zone installs a homeowner job: hang the head, drill the line-set opening, connect the line set, and have an electrician do the circuit. Multi-zone refrigerant handling, the vacuum pull, and charge weighing are not that job.
