Best mini split for heating and cooling

Published November 17, 2026

For a mini split that does both jobs, the spec sheet's SEER2, EER2 and HSPF2 tell you how it ranks against ENERGY STAR's lines. This page does not rank brands.

Quick answer: ENERGY STAR certifies split-system heat pumps, ductless mini splits included, at 15.2 SEER2, 11.0 EER2 and 7.8 HSPF2 or better. Its Most Efficient 2025 tier asks for 16.0 SEER2, 11.0 EER2 and 8.0 HSPF2, plus a COP of at least 1.75 and 45% of rated heating capacity at 5°F. These are efficiency floors, not sizing.

The certified lines are from ENERGY STAR Heat Pump Key Product Criteria (split systems), read November 17, 2026. The Most Efficient 2025 lines are the “Split system HP (Ducted & Ductless)” row and the non-cold-climate 5°F backstop in the ENERGY STAR Most Efficient 2025 cover letter (December 4, 2024), read the same day. No newer Most Efficient heat pump tier was found on ENERGY STAR's pages read that day.

Check a spec sheet

Enter the three ratings from the model's spec sheet or AHRI certificate. The 5°F fields are optional; they only matter for the Most Efficient tier.

Pass/fail compares each number you type with the lines in the table below. The capacity percent is 5°F capacity ÷ 47°F capacity × 100, derived on this page. A model can meet every line and still not be on ENERGY STAR's certified list; the label comes from certification, not from this check.

Heating and cooling, one machine

SEER2 and EER2 are the cooling half. HSPF2 is the heating half. A unit that is strong on one and weak on the other can still miss ENERGY STAR, because the split-system line needs all three. For winters where the 5°F number decides comfort, ENERGY STAR's Cold Climate lines (70% capacity at 5°F) are a separate, stricter test; they are on best mini split for cold climates.

Rated SEER2 values for common mini split sizes are on the mini split SEER2 chart, and the ENERGY STAR rows this site has collected are on ENERGY STAR sizing data. Size comes after efficiency: room Btu/h is on mini split room size, and heating versus cooling load is on heat pump sizing.
