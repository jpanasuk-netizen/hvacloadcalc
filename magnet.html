Print this calculator’s factors before you pick a head.

Oversizing feels safe. It isn't. A head that's too big short-cycles, leaves the room clammy, and dies early.

This calculator’s baseline is 25 BTU/hr per square foot. Then four factors: ceiling height, sun, insulation, and windows.

The steps. Sq ft × 25. × (ceiling ÷ 8). × sun factor. × insulation factor. + 600 BTU per window. + 10%, round up to the nearest 500. Then pick the next head in the calculator’s list.

The four knobs

FactorAdjustment
Ceiling heightmultiply by height ÷ 8 (10 ft ceiling = +25%)
Sun exposurevery sunny ×1.15 · average ×1.0 · shaded ×0.90
Insulationpoor ×1.25 · average ×1.0 · good ×0.85
Windows+600 BTU/hr each

A head far above the load is capacity you can't use.
