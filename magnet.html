DIY mini-split · one page
9k

The bedroom almost always wants a 9k head. Not a 18k because it was on sale.

Oversizing feels safe. It isn't. A head that's too big short-cycles, leaves the room clammy, and dies early.

Baseline is 20–30 BTU/hr per square foot (25 in the middle), from the same Manual-J-inspired rule of thumb on the calculator. Then four published adjustments — ceiling height, sun, insulation, windows.

Use it in 10 minutes. Sq ft × 25. × (ceiling ÷ 8). × sun factor. × insulation factor. + 600 BTU per window. + 10%, round up to the nearest 500. Then pick the next standard head size.

Past ~30% above load you're paying for capacity you can't use. Undersizing just runs longer on the worst days — cheaper mistake.
