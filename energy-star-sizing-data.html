ENERGY STAR room AC and dehumidifier sizing data

ENERGY STAR sizes a room air conditioner at 5,000 BTU/h for 100 to 150 sq ft, 12,000 for 450 to 550 sq ft and 34,000 for 2,000 to 2,500 sq ft, assuming 8 ft ceilings. Add 10% for a very sunny room, 600 BTU/h per extra person and 4,000 BTU/h in a kitchen.

Last updated: October 10, 2026.

Built by Jeremy Panasuk.

The area rows are ENERGY STAR's room air conditioner sizing for a single room with 8 ft ceilings. They are not a whole-house Manual J load. The dehumidifier rows are ENERGY STAR minimum capacities for portable dehumidifiers.
