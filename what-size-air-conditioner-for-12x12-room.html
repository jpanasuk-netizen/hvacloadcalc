What size air conditioner for a 12x12 room?

Quick answer: A 12 x 12 room is 144 sq ft, inside ENERGY STAR's 100 to 150 sq ft row: a 5,000 BTU/h room air conditioner, assuming 8 ft ceilings. ENERGY STAR says to cut 10% for a heavily shaded room, add 10% for a very sunny one and add 600 BTU/h per person beyond two.

About the 144 sq ft answer

12 ft × 12 ft = 144 sq ft, which is under the 150 sq ft line, so the chart starts at 5,000 BTU/h. A very sunny room adds 10% (5,500 BTU/h), and a third person adds 600 BTU/h. Small rooms on the edge of a row are covered in more detail on the 150 sq ft page. This is a room-unit chart, not a whole-house load calculation.
